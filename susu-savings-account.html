Susu Savings Account

Our susu account brings the bank to you. A mobile banker visits your shop, stall or home on agreed days and collects your contribution, so you save a little every day without leaving your work.

Who it is for

Market women, traders, artisans, drivers and anyone who earns daily.

What you get

  • A mobile banker collects at your place of work
  • Save any amount, as often as daily
  • Your money is held in a licensed bank, not with an individual collector
  • A good savings record can help you qualify for a micro loan

Plan your susu

See how small daily amounts add up.

You will have saved

Before any interest or fees. Ask your mobile banker about the terms for your account.

What you need

  • Your Ghana Card (required for every account since July 2022)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Your GhanaPost GPS address or proof of address

Bring the originals to the branch. Prepare with our checklists

How to start

  1. Send your detailsUse the form on this page, call +233 50 822 2743 or walk into any branch.
  2. We call youA member of staff calls within one working day to answer your questions and tell you exactly what to bring.
  3. Visit your branchBring your Ghana Card and the documents listed above.
  4. You are set upWe open your account or policy and explain how to use it, including U-Connect and mobile money.

Questions

How do I know my contribution is recorded?
Every collection is recorded in your susu card and on your account. Ask for your balance at any branch, or check on U-Connect.
Can I get a loan from my susu account?
Regular savers can apply for a Micro Loan. Your savings history is part of the assessment.
