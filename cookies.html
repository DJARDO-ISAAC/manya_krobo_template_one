In short

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and no third-party scripts.
  • Our fonts and images are served from our own website, not from other companies.
  • Your browser stores at most two small settings, and they never leave your device.
  • If we ever add analytics, we will ask for your consent first.

01What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves on your device. Local storage is a similar feature of your browser that lets a website remember a setting. Some are needed for a site to work; others track what you do across websites to show you advertising.

This website uses no cookies at all. It uses local storage only for the two settings described below.

02What we store

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
bank5-notice-[id]Local storageRemembers that you closed a site notice, so it does not appear again.Until you clear it
bank5-reviewLocal storageUsed by bank staff only. Remembers review mode, which highlights figures waiting for approval. It is only set if you open a page with ?review=1.Until you clear it or open a page with ?review=0

Neither setting contains personal information, and neither is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

03Other companies

We do not embed content from other companies, so no other company can set cookies while you are on this website. Our fonts and images are stored on our own server.

Some links take you to other services. Once you are there, their cookies and privacy notices apply:

  • Google Maps, for branch directions
  • WhatsApp and Facebook, if you contact us or share a page
  • The App Store and Google Play, to download U-Connect

04Forms on this website

When you fill in a form, what you type stays in your browser until you send it. We do not save half-finished forms. Read the privacy notice to see how we use the details you send us.

05How to remove what is stored

You can remove our settings at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser:

  • Chrome (Android and computer): Settings, Privacy and security, Site settings, View permissions and data stored across sites.
  • Safari (iPhone): Settings, Safari, Advanced, Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data, Manage Data.

The website works the same with or without these settings. A dismissed notice may simply appear again.

06If we add analytics

If we decide to measure how people use the website, we will update this page first and ask for your consent with a clear choice to say no. We will not use analytics to build advertising profiles.
