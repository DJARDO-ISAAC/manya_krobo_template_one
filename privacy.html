In short

  • We collect what we need to open and run your accounts, assess loans and meet our legal duties.
  • We never sell your information, and we never ask for your PIN or password.
  • We share it only with people who need it to serve you, or where the law requires it.
  • You can ask to see, correct or stop the use of your information at any time.

01Who we are

Manya Krobo Rural Bank PLC (“the bank”, “we”, “us”) is a rural and community bank with its head office at Head Office, Abanse, Odumase-Krobo, Eastern Region. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this notice, and we are registered with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Our Data Protection Supervisor oversees how we handle personal information. You can reach them at the head office, by email at info@manyakroboruralbank.com, or by post at P.O. Box 176, Odumase-Krobo.

02What this notice covers

This notice applies to personal information about customers and former customers, people who apply for our products, guarantors, shareholders, and anyone who contacts us or uses this website.

Some products, such as U-Connect, mobile money and insurance, have their own terms that explain extra details. Where they differ, the product terms apply to that product.

03What we collect

Information you give us

  • Identity: your name, date of birth, Ghana Card number, photographs and signature.
  • Contact: phone numbers, email address, residential and GhanaPost GPS address.
  • Financial: your occupation, employer, income, payslips and bank statements.
  • For loans: details of guarantors and collateral, and the purpose of the loan.
  • For insurance: details of what you want to cover, and beneficiaries.

Information we create when you bank with us

  • Account numbers, balances, transactions, loan repayments and correspondence.
  • If you use U-Connect: your registered phone number, device details and login history.
  • CCTV images in and around our branches, kept for security.
  • Recordings of calls to our hotline, where we tell you the call is recorded.

Information from other people

  • The National Identification Authority, to verify your Ghana Card.
  • Licensed credit reference bureaus, when you apply for credit.
  • Your employer, when you apply for a salary loan repaid by deduction from your pay.
  • MTN Mobile Money and the ARB Apex Bank, when you move money between services.

Information from this website

When you use a form on this website we collect your name, phone number, email address if you give it, your nearest branch and what you ask about. We do not ask for your PIN, password or full account number online. See the cookies page for what is stored on your device.

04How and why we use it

We only use your information where the law allows it. The table shows the main purposes and the legal reason for each.

PurposeLegal reason
Opening and running your accounts, loans and policiesTo perform our contract with you
Checking your identity and screening for money laundering and fraudLegal duty, including the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2020 (Act 1044)
Assessing whether you can afford a loanTo perform our contract, and our legitimate interest in lending responsibly
Keeping our branches, systems and your money secureLegal duty and legitimate interest
Reporting to the Bank of Ghana and other regulatorsLegal duty
Telling you about changes to your account or our servicesTo perform our contract
Telling you about new products and offersYour consent, which you can withdraw at any time
Improving our products, service and websiteOur legitimate interest

05Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only when we need to, and only with:

  • Our staff, who are trained in confidentiality and only see what their role requires.
  • The ARB Apex Bank, which provides clearing, payment and digital banking services to rural and community banks.
  • MTN Mobile Money, when you link your account to your wallet.
  • Our partner insurer, when you ask about or take out insurance.
  • Licensed credit reference bureaus, under the Credit Reporting Act, 2007 (Act 726).
  • Your employer, to arrange deductions for a salary loan you have agreed to.
  • Regulators and authorities such as the Bank of Ghana, the Financial Intelligence Centre and the Ghana Revenue Authority, and courts or the police when the law requires it.
  • Our auditors, lawyers and other professional advisers, who must keep it confidential.

Anyone who processes information on our behalf must protect it to the same standard we do, and may only use it on our instructions.

06Transfers outside Ghana

We store your information in Ghana. If a service provider needs to process it in another country, we make sure it is protected to the standard required by the Data Protection Act before we transfer it.

07How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it, or as long as the law requires.

InformationHow long
Account and transaction recordsAt least 5 years after the account closes, as required by anti-money laundering law
Loan filesAt least 5 years after the loan is fully repaid
Website enquiries that do not lead to an account12 months
CCTV recordings30 days, unless needed for an investigation
Complaint records5 years from when the complaint is closed

08How we protect it

We use physical, technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Access controls so that staff see only what they need.
  • Encryption of data sent between your phone and U-Connect.
  • Secure storage of paper records at the branch and head office.
  • Regular staff training and checks on how information is handled.

We will never ask for your PIN, password or the codes we send you, by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email. If anyone does, it is fraud. See banking safely.

09Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to:

  • See the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer have a reason to keep.
  • Object to us using your information for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting what we did before.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission.

To use any of these rights, visit any branch with your Ghana Card, email info@manyakroboruralbank.com or write to the Data Protection Supervisor at P.O. Box 176, Odumase-Krobo. We may ask you to prove who you are before we act. We aim to respond within 21 days, and there is no charge.

Some information we must keep by law, for example records needed to prevent money laundering, even if you ask us to delete it. If so, we will explain why.

10Children

We open School Child Accounts for children with a parent or guardian, who gives consent and manages the account on the child's behalf until the child is old enough to operate it. We do not knowingly collect information from children through this website.

11How we make decisions

Loan and account decisions are made by our staff. We may use scores from credit reference bureaus to help, but a person always reviews the decision, and you can ask us to explain it.

12Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the law or our services change. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you at your branch, by SMS or on this website before it takes effect.

13Contact and complaints

If you have a question or are unhappy with how we have handled your information, contact the Data Protection Supervisor at info@manyakroboruralbank.com or +233 50 822 2743, or follow our complaints process.

If you are still not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana at dataprotection.org.gh.
